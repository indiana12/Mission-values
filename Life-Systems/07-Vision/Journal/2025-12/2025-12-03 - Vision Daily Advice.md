## Formula / Framework
- **Name**: Family Equity Ratio (FER).
- **Expression**: `FER = Rituals Honoured / Rituals Promised` (week). Target ≥ 0.9.
- **Where to log**: Life Systems Hub → Family Ledger card nightly.

## Scenario Split
- **Positive**: FER tracked → rituals blocked → nieces bond with you → grandparents extend stay → you gain calm mornings.
- **Negative**: FER ignored → rituals missed → elders leave early → household help collapses during Q4.
- **Levers**: Calendar block, asynchronous client updates, hydration + snack ritual before family time.
