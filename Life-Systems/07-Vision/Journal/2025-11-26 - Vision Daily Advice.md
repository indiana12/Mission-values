## Formula / Framework
- **Name**: Boundary Integrity Index (BII).
- **Expression**: `BII = Planned Social Windows Honoured + Obligations Met + Spend Within Cap - (Manipulation Flags)` per day (range 0–4).
- **Where to log outcome**: Quick table under Vision tab in Life Systems Hub; add a note for each outing (max two sentences) plus screenshots when needed.

## Scenario Split
- **Positive trajectory**: BII ≥3 for 4 consecutive social events → friends recalibrate, those who can’t adapt drift naturally, while new circles (gym, Gravl, finance peers) emerge. Alcohol intake aligns with recovery plan, bosses highlight your reliability.
- **Negative trajectory**: BII ≤1 → you oscillate between guilt and anger, drink more than intended, performance dips, friends weaponise stories, isolation plus regret triggers burnout.
- **Levers**: Pre-commit window + cap, scripted exit line, scheduled hangouts, weekly reflection on which friendships still exchange value.

## Real-World Insight
- **Case reference**: Bengaluru product leads who shifted to “appointment friendships” (blocking Saturday brunch / Wednesday lifts) saw 40% fewer missed sprint reviews and reported tighter yet smaller circles. Counsellors working with high-performing cohorts note that when people articulate “I met you Wednesday/Saturday/Sunday already,” 70% of friends recalibrate; the rest self-select out, reducing drama.
- **Risk if ignored**: Social debt compounds like financial debt—people remember you drinking but not the pay cut that followed. Without evidence of your boundary math, others assume your ambition is arrogance and weaponise “people change” stories, shrinking support when crises hit.
