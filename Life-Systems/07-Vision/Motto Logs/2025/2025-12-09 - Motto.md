# 2025-12-09 Motto Entry

- **Theme × Pillar**: Focus × Ops (MetroMule deck)
- **Inspiration source**: Random prompt "raindrop metronome"

## Quote
> “The shortest way to do many things is to do only one thing at a time.”
> — Mozart

## Reframe
Rain hitting the window is a metronome: it begs you to stay on one beat. Today that beat is the corporate deck; everything else can ride the next bar.

## Discipline Action (today)
Mute every app except the deck tab for 90 minutes. If a new demand comes in, jot it on a sticky note, not in your brain.

## Visualization / Mantra
Picture the rain as a metronome clicking on your desk. *“One beat, one deliverable.”*

## Upscale Prompt
- Question: Which single deliverable, done well, makes three other tasks irrelevant today?
