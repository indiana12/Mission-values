## Script Capsule (choose one per day)
- **Format**: Reel/Short — “AI Filmed My Day”
- **Hook**: “I let AI decide what I film today.”
- **Prep**:
  1. Open ChatGPT and type: “You are a cinematic director. Give me 5 specific shots to film today to tell the story of a founder balancing family + FloodHive ops. Include sensory cues.”
  2. Copy the shot list into Notes/Notion.
  3. Keep phone, mic, and editing app (CapCut) ready.
- **Shoot**:
  1. Follow each AI shot exactly. Example: “Shot 1: capture the coffee drip sound” → record that POV with ASMR audio.
  2. Add on-screen text quoting the AI instruction for each clip.
  3. Capture B-roll if needed (drone batteries, family moments, journal).
- **Edit**:
  1. Sequence clips in the AI order, add subtitle commentary (“AI told me to film this tool sound”).
  2. Overlay calm lo-fi audio + trending GRWM track.
  3. Insert hook slide at start, CTA at end.
- **Body beats**:
  1. Establish: Show the prompt + Shot 1.
  2. Journey: Shots 2–4 with quick insights.
  3. Outcome: Shot 5 reflection — “Did AI improve the day?”
- **CTA / Monetisation**: “Drop a prompt to control tomorrow’s vlog” + link to “100 AI Shot Prompts” Notion page (sell or email capture). Add gear affiliate links if relevant.
- **Checklist**:
  - [ ] Prompt saved
  - [ ] 5 clips filmed
  - [ ] ASMR audio captured
  - [ ] CTA inserted
