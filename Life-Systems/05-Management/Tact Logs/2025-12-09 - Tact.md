## Script Capsule
- **Format**: YouTube Short / Reel
- **Hook**: “We drew random chits to plan a ₹1,000 family bonding day.”
- **Body beats**:
  1. POV shot drawing chits (meal, chore, mini-trip). Text: “Budget ₹1,000. Every chit = a task to do together.”
  2. Montage: cooking ₹300 meal, playing ₹200 board game, ₹300 bus ride, ₹200 treat. Quick banter + subtitles.
  3. Closing: highlight takeaway (“Best ₹1,000 spent—memories > malls”) + ask viewers for next chit idea.
- **CTA / Monetisation**: Link to a downloadable “Family Bonding Chit Kit” (Notion/PDF) or sponsor a budget-friendly brand (UPI app, grocery, board game). Could later sell a mentorship challenge (“30-day Everyday Rituals”).
- **Suggested agents**: Jarvis (money + idea), Motto (discipline/family), Vision (storyline), Blackrock (brand tie-ins).

## Monetisation Angles / Ideas
- Release a paid “Ritual Kit” (templates, prompts, chit ideas) for other families/creators.
- Pitch budget brands (digital wallets, quick commerce) for integration (“sponsored ₹1,000 challenge”).
- Start a recurring series “Random Chit Days” and build an email list with behind-the-scenes tips (upsell to mentorship/community).

## Notes / Follow-ups
- Keep filming style simple (handheld, warm color grade, natural sound). The niche loves authenticity, not cinematic edits.
- Track comment suggestions; they become future episodes and product ideas.
