# Nutrition Overview

[[Life-Systems/01-Fitness/Fitness|← Fitness]]

## Daily Targets (Oct 2025 Metrics)
- Calories: 1,700–1,850 kcal (supports ~0.7 kg/week fat loss).
- Protein: 130–150 g (preserve SMM 48.4 kg).
- Carbs: ~100 g focused around workouts/swim.
- Fats: ~40 g emphasizing omega-3, olive oil, nuts.
- Hydration: 4.5 L water + electrolytes; magnesium + Ashwagandha + Omega-3 at night.

## Meal Framework
| Meal | Components | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lunch (main) | 200 g lean protein + rice/roti + ½ plate veggies + curd/bone soup | Grilled chicken + rice + sautéed veggies |
| Post-swim snack | Whey + fruit + almonds/peanut butter | Whey shake + kiwi + almonds |
| Dinner (light) | 150 g paneer/eggs + veggies + olive oil | Paneer stir fry + salad |

## Cost & Budget Plan (Nov 2025 intake)
Assumptions: grocery prices in Hyderabad (BigBasket/Pista House), whey tub ₹12,000/75 scoops, seasonal fruit costs, olive oil ₹1,000/L. Numbers help Blackrock sync monthly food spend.

| Slot | Portions based on routine | Approx. cost (₹) | Notes / swaps if budget tight |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| **Pre-workout** | 60 g muesli or oats (₹35) + banana (₹10) + 40 g blueberries (₹30) | 75 | Swap blueberries for papaya/jamun to drop ₹20. |
| **Post-workout** | Dymatize ISO100 (1 scoop) + water | 160 | Buy 5 lb tub on sale → ₹135 per scoop. |
| **Lunch** | 200 g chicken/fish/paneer + rice/rotis + veggies + curd | 200 | Batch cook on Sunday to shave ₹30/day. |
| **Post-lunch hydration** | Homemade buttermilk 300 ml + lemon/electrolyte pinch | 15 | DIY keeps sodium/potassium ratio on target. |
| **Pre-swim snack** | Coconut water 250 ml + banana (share from breakfast) | 25 | Skip if RHR low; else stick for electrolytes. |
| **Post-swim** | 150 g yogurt (₹25) + 20 g almonds (₹18) + seasonal fruit (₹30) | 73 | Buy almonds in 1 kg bag to cut to ₹15. |
| **Dinner** | Olive oil + egg salad (Pista House) incl. veggies | 250 | Replicate at home thrice/week to cut to ₹160. |
| **Hydration / extras** | 4.5 L water + electrolyte sachet | 20 | Electral-lite or Fast&Up sachets. |

**Daily food cost ≈ ₹818** → Weekly ₹5,726 → 30-day cycle ₹24,540.

Supplement stack (collagen, creatine, ZMA, Omega-3, Ashwagandha, L-Carnitine cycle) averages **₹4,500/month** when bought in quarterly bundles. Share these numbers with Blackrock so Defender/Home fund projections include nutrition essentials.

## Supplement Stack
- Morning: Collagen + lemon water.
- Pre-workout: L-Carnitine 500–1000 mg + coffee (cycle 6–8 weeks on, 2 off).
- Post-workout: Whey + creatine 5 g.
- Night: ZMA + Omega-3 + Ashwagandha.
