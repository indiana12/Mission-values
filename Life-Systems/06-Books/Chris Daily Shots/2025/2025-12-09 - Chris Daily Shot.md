## Author’s Core Idea
Build assets under your control even when you have a day job. Treat your time, knowledge, and side ventures as your “business,” not the employer’s.

## Mindmap Snapshot
- Central: Build assets while employed.
- Support 1: Track personal financial statements.
- Support 2: Invest time in learning sales/accounting.
- Guardrail: Don’t inflate lifestyle with each raise.
