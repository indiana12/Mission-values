## Author’s Core Idea
Managers must focus on leverage—activities that multiply output through others or systems. Every meeting/task should be audited for leverage.

## Mindmap Snapshot
- Central: Leverage activities.
- Support 1: Delegation/teaching.
- Support 2: Process design.
- Guardrail: Low-leverage tasks should be automated/deleted.

## Visualization Layer

## Real-Life Application
Example: Instead of answering repeat Tact requests manually, create a template + Loom—one action saves dozens of minutes later.
