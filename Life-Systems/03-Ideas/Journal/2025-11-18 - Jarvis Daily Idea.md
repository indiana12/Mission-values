# 2025-11-18 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Urban commuting + wellness infrastructure
- Why now: Hyderabad traffic averaging 20 km/h; metro feeders overstretched; professionals want active commuting without sweat; GHMC investing in last-mile pilots.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: GlideDeck Commute Lounges
- One-liner: Solar-powered elevated walkways with moving sidewalks, mist-cooled rest pods, and micro-mobility docks connecting metro stations to business districts.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: PPP contracts + advertising + premium commuter subscriptions (₹999/month for priority access + locker services).
- Unit economics: ₹3.4 cr capex per 500 m deck; O&M ₹4.5 L/month; revenue mix 50% govt annuity, 30% ads, 20% member services.
- Scaling levers: Bundle with corporate CSR, sell data insights (footfall, air quality), franchise maintenance to facility firms.
