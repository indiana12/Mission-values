# Jarvis Adaptation Backlog

Use this sheet to park real-world references (reels, podcasts, friends’ stories). Every entry must link to a need already validated elsewhere; Jarvis will reshuffle them for future “idea of the day” prompts.

## How to log
1. Capture the **source** (city/operator/video link).
2. Describe the **need proven** (data point, behaviour, revenue figure, public policy).
3. Note **India fit levers** (policy tweaks, infra partners, cultural shifts, price points).
4. Mark **Status**: `scan`, `research`, `prototype`, `launched`.
