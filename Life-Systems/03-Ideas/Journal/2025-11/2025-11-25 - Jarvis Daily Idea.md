# 2025-11-25 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Smart micro-warehousing + curbside fulfillment for ONDC/quick-commerce
- Why now (India context): Tier-1 streets getting vehicle restrictions; ONDC orders rising 70% QoQ; kiranas lack digital inventory; municipalities want organized curb space; EV gig fleets need predictable hubs.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: FluxPort Curb Pods
- One-liner: AI-routed curbside pods that combine modular warehousing, EV charging, and outsourced pick-pack staff so brands and kiranas can fulfill ONDC orders within 20 minutes without clogging streets.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: Pod lease (₹2.2 L/month), throughput fee (₹4/order), EV charging margin (₹2/kWh), and data subscription for inventory insights.
- Unit economics: Pod capex ₹85 L; handles 1,800 orders/day; EBITDA 27% at 70% utilisation.
- Scaling levers: Franchise with logistics firms, integrate with municipal PPPs, offer white-label pods to marketplaces, embed AI routing for fleets.

## Regulatory & Ease-of-Business
- Approvals: Municipal PPP licence, parking lease, fire safety, FSSAI (if food), pollution NOC for batteries.
- Hurdles: Vendor politics, hawker associations, night logistics permits.
- Mitigation: Offer revenue share to city corporations, create hawker inclusion programs, implement silent EV fleets.

## Operational Readiness
- Installation: 14 days (prefab pod drop 4, shelving 3, IT + sensors 4, training 3).
- Staff & SOP: Pod captain, pick-pack crew, EV tech, data analyst (remote). SOP covers slot booking, hygiene, safety.
- IoT metrics: Inventory turnover, dwell time, EV uptime, curb occupancy, fulfilment SLA.
