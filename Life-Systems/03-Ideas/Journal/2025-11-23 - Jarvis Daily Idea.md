# 2025-11-23 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Net-zero housing retrofits + circular construction marketplaces
- Why now (India context): Urban housing stock is aging; states (MH, KA, TS) offering property-tax rebates for green upgrades; RERA + ESG reporting push developers to show sustainability metrics; middle-class residents want lower power bills and better indoor air quality.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: ReLife Retrofit Exchange
- One-liner: A marketplace + on-site micro-factory that turns demolition waste into modular retrofit panels so apartment societies can upgrade to net-zero, low-maintenance homes within 12 weeks.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: Design + retrofit package fee (₹1,900/sq ft), recycled-material supply margin (18%), and Maintenance-as-a-Service (₹9/sq ft annually) for IoT monitoring.
- Unit economics / pricing: Average 40,000 sq ft society; revenue ₹7.6 cr/project; gross margin 30% after fabrication + labour.
- Scaling levers: State-wide franchise partners (civil contractors + ESG funds), carbon-credit monetisation, white-label deals with large developers for their old towers.

## Regulatory & Ease-of-Business
- Key approvals: RERA retrofit clearance, municipal building permits, waste transport licences, pollution control board nod for micro-factory.
- City hurdles: Resident welfare consent (67%+), night-work restrictions, debris logistics in dense lanes.
- Mitigation plan: Offer legal + financing desk to RWAs, use EV mini-tippers for waste hauling, schedule quiet-hour workblocks.

## Operational Readiness
- Installation / rollout: 12 weeks (audit 2, design 3, fabrication 4, install 3).
- Staff & SOP: Retrofit architects, structural engineers, site supervisors, circular-material technologists, IoT team.
- IoT metrics: Energy savings %, air-quality index, water recycling rate, waste diversion %, carbon credits generated.
