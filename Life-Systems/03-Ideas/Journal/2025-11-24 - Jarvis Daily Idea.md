# 2025-11-24 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Hospital-to-home transition logistics + telehealth command centres
- Why now (India context): 65% of tertiary hospital beds are overbooked; Ayushman Bharat and private insurers push for shorter admissions; elder-care demand rising in Tier-1/2; ONDC and drone corridors enable regulated medical deliveries.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: MediSpoke Continuity Pods
- One-liner: Containerized discharge hubs outside hospitals that combine telehealth, pharma compounding, and drone-ready logistics so patients can leave within 12 hours while still getting ICU-grade monitoring at home.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: Per-discharge package (₹18k average), remote monitoring subscription (₹6k/month), white-label operations fee for hospitals (₹4L/month/pod).
- Unit economics / pricing: Pod capex ₹1.6 cr; handles 180 discharges/month; EBITDA 24% after staffing + leasing.
- Scaling levers: Partner with hospital chains, insurers, home-care startups; franchise pods near clusters; integrate with ONDC Health for last-mile delivery.

## Regulatory & Ease-of-Business
- Key approvals: CDSCO for compounding, Pharmacy licence, DGCA drone ops, state health NOC, biomedical waste handling.
- City hurdles: Parking/land for pods, hospital tie-ups, data privacy compliance.
- Mitigation plan: Bundle compliance kit, offer revenue share to hospitals, anonymise EMR data via secure API.

## Operational Readiness
- Installation / rollout: 21 days (site prep 5, pod drop 4, calibration 5, training 7).
- Staff & SOP: Nurse leads, pharmacy tech, logistics dispatcher, telehealth doctor, drone pilot.
- IoT metrics: Vitals uptime, SLA for med deliveries, drone success rate, readmission %, patient NPS.

- Urban vs rural: 85/15 (start urban, expand via drones).
- Purchasing power mix: Lower 20 / Middle 45 / Upper 25 / Ultra 10.
- Emotional drivers: Avoid infections, stay with family, predictable care, lower bills.
