## Operator (CA Cousin) Lens
- **Core mindset**: Protect partner capital through clean compliance, maintain steady cashflow, and ensure the venue stays bankable for future refinancing or expansion.
- **Daily toolkit**: GST filings for mandap services, TDS for contractors, fire/commercial licenses, insurance coordination, banker relations, booking contracts.
- **Leverage**: Trusted advisor for wealthy families; becomes the nerve center for bookings, payments, and audits—hence earns equity despite lower cash infusion.

## Mindset & Risk Considerations
- **Landowner**: Wants airtight title protections, insurance, and clarity on exit valuations if project sold/refinanced.
- **Finance/CA**: Focus on GST classification (mandap + catering), depreciation, dividend vs. salary mix, ensuring enough free cash to service debt if taken.
- **Hospitality partners**: Evaluate occupancy rates, uniqueness (VIP access near Kokapet towers), and upgrade cycles to keep ahead of luxury expectations.
- **Operational risks**: Structural integrity (fabrication spans), fire safety, parking management, high-profile guest security, event scheduling vs. noise regulations.

## Due-Diligence / Fine-Tuning Tasks
1. **Cap table review**: Obtain LLP/Company agreement to confirm stakes, land valuation, capital-call rules, exit clauses.
2. **Demand sheet**: Benchmark against other Kokapet-area conventions (rates, occupancy, differentiators) to stress-test revenue assumptions.
3. **P&L template**: Build event-driven model (hall rate, add-ons, room uptake) vs. fixed costs (staff, utilities, maintenance, debt service).
4. **Risk register**: Map structural, regulatory, partner, and reputational risks; assign owner per risk with mitigation.
5. **Governance plan**: Define how 8 partners vote (simple majority vs. supermajority), disputes resolution, reporting cadence.
6. **Architectural/Topo scan**: Capture drone/360 views of hall span, lawn drainage, kitchen flow, and VIP ingress/egress to quantify capacity caps (cars/hour, guest count, emergency exits) before approving expansions or sub-leases.

## Field Intelligence Plan
- **Architectural read**: Drone/topo walkthrough, note span, load-bearing patterns, drainage, kitchen flow, VIP entrances; sketch from memory to practise “read-the-building” skills.
- **Contractor chatter**: Chat with fabrication crews, lift installers, kitchen vendors—casual talk about deliveries usually reveals cost overruns, payment terms, and who’s funding what.
- **Booking signals**: Photograph whiteboards/calendars, observe parking-use patterns, listen for planner names to estimate pipeline without asking directly.
- **Financial scent**: Look for bank logos on gates, mortgage placards, or NBFC stickers on equipment. Note recurring vendor names to cross-check MCA filings later.
- **Partner persona mapping**: Note body language in meetings, who gives instructions, who signs cheques. Build an empathy map (goal, fear, leverage) for each persona based solely on observable behaviour.

## Decision Tree – Reading the Deal
```

  Yes → Capture lender name → Check if EMI math (loan size, rate) fits observed bookings. 
    Are bookings calendar > 3 months full? 
      Yes → Debt likely covered → Watch for expansion signals. 
      No → Look for marketing pushes, rate cuts, or partner cash infusions.
  No → Look for signs of land-for-equity or private funding.

      Yes → Land-dominant structure → Expect priority returns. 
      No → Silent backer or cash-heavy partner; watch who handles VIPs.

Next → Are vendors paid on time? (Ask casually about payment cycles.) 
  If complaints / cash payments → Possible layered capital or stress. 
  If smooth / electronic → Clean capex, easier to attract lenders.

Next → Observe booking board. 
  >12 weekends booked → Cashflow strong → Partners may leverage asset. 
  <6 weekends → Risk of cash crunch → Expect aggressive sales or rate changes.

Final → Map partner roles from behaviour. 
  Who stands with politicians? → Political capital holder. 
  Who tracks invoices? → Finance lead (your cousin). 
  Who walks vendors? → Ops lead. 
Use this mapping to update stakeholder share scenario assumptions.
```

## Generalisation Framework (for other venues)
- **Data schema**: Capture uniform fields—acreage, built-up area, room count, parking capacity, VIP access, booking calendar, event categories, tariff bands, compliance dates.
- **Observation cadence**: Schedule monthly site visits per venue (weekday + weekend), log guest counts, vendor presence, upgrades, notices.
- **Benchmark dashboard**: Convert observations into comparable metrics (estimated revenue, occupancy %, reinvestment spend, compliance score) to rank venues.
- **Vendor/Planner graph**: Map which decorators/caterers/planners operate across sites to identify gatekeepers and distribution partners.
- **Opportunity flags**: Define triggers (e.g., bookings <50% for 2 months, repeated regulatory notices) that signal deals needing tech/compliance help.
- **Productization**: Package insights as “Venue Dossiers” for investors/owners—earning fees or equity for delivering intelligence.
