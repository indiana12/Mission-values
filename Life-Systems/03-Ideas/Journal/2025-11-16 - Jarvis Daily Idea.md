# 2025-11-16 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Urban climate tech × community infrastructure
- Why now (India context): IMD projects longer heatwaves across Telangana; Hyderabad GHMC mandating rooftop rainwater systems by 2027; corporates chasing ESG credits.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: HeatShield Rooftop Pods
- One-liner: Modular, solar-powered pergola pods that convert unused rooftops into cooled community lounges while harvesting rainwater and filtering greywater for reuse.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: Design-build-install packages for apartments, coworking spaces, premium gyms.
- Unit economics / pricing: Avg ₹28 L per 1,000 sq. ft. pod (materials 45%, labour 20%, IoT & solar 15%, margin 20%).
- Scaling levers: Annual maintenance contracts (₹2.5 L/pod), sensor data platform subscription for ESG reporting, franchised installers in tier-2 cities.

## Regulatory & Ease-of-Business
- Key approvals/licences: Structural stability certificate, GHMC rooftop usage permit, CEIG for solar tie-in, water board approval for reuse.
- City/state hurdles: Load limits on old apartments; need to coordinate with society bylaws.
- Mitigation plan: Offer structural audit partner, bundle permit filing, maintain 5-year liability insurance.

## Operational Readiness
- Installation / rollout time: 45 days (design 10, fabrication 20, install 15).
- Staff & SOP notes: Core team of architect, civil lead, sustainability officer, IoT engineer; rely on prefab manufacturing partner.
- IoT / monitoring metrics: Surface temperature delta, solar yield, rain capture, occupancy analytics through privacy-safe counters.

## Sustainability & ESG Levers
- Environmental impact: Each pod captures ~120k litres rainwater/year, reduces rooftop temp by 6–8 °C, supplies 5 kW solar offset.
- Social impact: Creates shaded commons for residents/staff, employs local fabricators, dedicates 1 pod per 10 sold to govt schools via CSR.
- Governance: Publish quarterly ESG dashboard; third-party audit on water reuse compliance.
- Measurement plan: Sensors stream to dashboard; KPIs = litres harvested, kWh generated, occupancy hours.
