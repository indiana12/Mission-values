# 2025-12-11 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: “Neighborhood skill guilds” for creators + freelancers (micro-cohorts with revenue share).
- Why now: Creator economy decentralising; coworking expensive; residents want skill exchange and local gigs; ONDC allows hyperlocal service listings.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea: GuildStreet Circles.
- One-liner: Apartment/township-based micro guilds offering skill swaps, monthly challenges, and automated monetisation (handbook, templates, gigs).
- Need proven by: Creator houses, mastermind groups, community skill exchanges.
- Reference operator: Nas Company, OnDeck, Indie Hackers.
- Adaptation lever: Use society apps + UPI micro-payments + ONDC listing.
- Gap: No structured, local creator circles with recurring revenue and accountability.

## Business Model
- Subscription ₹999/month per member; 5% commission on guild-run gigs; brand workshops.
- Unit economics: 30-member guild per society = ₹30k MRR; Opex (community lead, tools) ₹12k → margin ₹18k.
