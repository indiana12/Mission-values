# 2025-12-12 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Micro “data guardian” collectives for neighborhoods (privacy + compliance services for residents & small businesses).
- Why now: DPDP Act enforcement in 2026; residents + RWAs clueless about compliance; opportunity to create community-run data vaults + consultancy.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: Datacare Commons.
- One-liner: Neighborhood cooperatives offering DPDP audits, secure vaults, and shared compliance officers for residents, RWAs, and local SMEs.
- Need proven by: EU GDPR community clinics, credit union models, data lockers like DigiLocker.
- Reference operator: DigiLocker, OneTrust (enterprise), legal clinics.
- Adaptation lever: Blend community trust with lightweight tech; partner with local lawyers and IT grads.
- Gap: Accessible, affordable DPDP help at community level.

## Business Model
- Subscription ₹299/month per household for secure vault + alerts; ₹2,499/month for small biz compliance package; training workshops ₹999.
- Unit economics: 200 households + 30 SMEs per neighborhood → ₹10 L MRR; Opex ₹4 L (staff, infra) → margin ₹6 L.
