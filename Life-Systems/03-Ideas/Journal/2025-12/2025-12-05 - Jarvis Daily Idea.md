# 2025-12-05 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Mixed-reality guild labs that re-skill industrial workers for EV, aerospace, and electronics contracts without shutting down plants.
- Why now (India context): PLI schemes for electronics/EVs demand 200k+ skilled operators by 2027; Karnataka + TN plan micro skill hubs but budgets lag. Shop floors can’t afford downtime for training; legacy ITIs lack real machines.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: GuildSim Micro Labs.
- One-liner: Pop-up mixed-reality labs inside industrial clusters that let workers practice EV battery assembly, PCB soldering, or aerospace riveting via haptic rigs and AI coaches tied to certification.
- Need proven by: Airbus VR training cut errors 40%; Tata Elxsi’s VR labs for automotive lines; Singapore polytechnics using MR for welders.
- Reference market/operator: AjnaLens, Simulanis, Holosuit, BYD training pods.
- Adaptation lever for India: Retrofit unused sheds in clusters (Peenya, Sriperumbudur) with modular rigs financed via CSR/PLI credits; integrate NSDC curriculum + ONDC credential wallets.
- India gap: MSMEs and Tier-2 suppliers can’t access expensive OEM academies or send staff to metros for weeks.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: ₹2,499 per worker certification, ₹1.2 L/month subscription for OEM cohorts (50 seats), ₹4 L setup fee for on-site pop-ups, content licensing to ITIs.
- Unit economics: Each lab = 6 rigs × 12 sessions/day = 72 seats. Blended ARPU ₹2,200 → ₹1.58 L/day → ₹47 L/month; costs ₹22 L (rent, trainers, amortised hardware) → 53% gross margin.
- Scaling levers: OEM co-branded academies, state subsidies, export training for Middle East clients.

## Regulatory & Ease-of-Business
- Approvals: NSDC affiliation, safety compliance for MR rigs, labour dept permission for after-hours training, OEM NDAs.
- Hurdles: Import duties on haptic hardware, union worries over automation.
- Mitigation: Local manufacturing of rigs, co-create modules with unions, show placement stats.

## Operational Readiness
- Timeline: 6 weeks for equipment procurement, 10 days for site prep, 1 week for instructor onboarding.
- Staff: 1 lab manager, 3 master trainers, 2 tech support, remote AI coach supervisor.
- IoT metrics: Session completion, error rate, biofeedback (hand tremor), job placement data.
