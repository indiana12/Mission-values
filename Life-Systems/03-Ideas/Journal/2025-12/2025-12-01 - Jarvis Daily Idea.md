# 2025-12-01 Jarvis Daily Idea

## Topic
- Sector: Cyclone-resilient solar cold rings for small-scale fishing harbours along India’s east coast.
- Why now (India context): Bay of Bengal cyclones now touch land 1.5× more often than a decade ago, wiping 30–40% of catch value due to spoilage. PMMSY and Sagarmala both released new harbour-modernisation grants this quarter, but co-ops still lack plug-and-play infrastructure they can deploy before the next storm season.

## Concept Synopsis
- Idea name: HarborShield Cold Rings.
- One-liner: Flood-proof, modular cold-storage “rings” that wrap busy harbour walls, run on rooftop solar + thermal batteries, and bundle insurance + market access for fisher collectives.
- Need proven by (data / geography / anecdote): FAO-backed cold cubes in Tamil Nadu lifted incomes 18%; Promethean Power rapid chillers showed 35% lower diesel use in dairy; Philippines’ “cold hub” reefers kept fish loss <5% in typhoon belts.
- Reference market / operator: Promethean Power Systems, Inficold, TESSOL, and Vietnam’s solar ice plants.
- Adaptation lever for India: Blend PMMSY subsidy (60%), SIDBI Blue Economy debt (6% interest), and CSR-backed resilience bonds. Use ONDC for coastal fish auctions and ONDC Logistics partners for overnight intercity dispatch.
- India gap we’re filling (what’s missing today): Harbour sheds still rely on diesel gensets placed on ground level; floods ruin motors and stocks. There’s no insured, elevated, sensor-led cold chain that co-ops can own.

## Business Model & Monetization
- Core revenue: ₹12/kg cold storage fee, ₹499/month sensor + dashboard seat, 2% success fee on ONDC auction trades routed through the platform.
- Unit economics / pricing: Each ring = 6 pods × 3 MT each = 18 MT capacity. At 70% utilisation and 2.2 turns/day → 27.7 MT/day × ₹12 = ₹3.32 L monthly gross. After energy + staffing (~₹1.05 L) and maintenance (~₹35k), operating surplus ≈₹1.92 L per ring. Capex ₹48 L (structure, refrigeration, solar canopy) → 26-month payback before subsidies.
- Scaling levers: Bundle weather-indexed insurance (premium share), issue carbon credits for diesel displacement, franchise operations via harbour federations.

## Regulatory & Ease-of-Business
- Key approvals/licences: Harbour board MoU, electricity safety clearance, FSSAI storage license, net metering permit, environmental consent to operate.
- City/state hurdles: Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) limits on permanent foundations, fishing department union politics, high upfront insurance requirements.
- Mitigation plan: Use elevated, prefab steel that qualifies as temporary, offer revenue-share with harbour boards, and pool insurance via NABARD-backed mutual.

## Operational Readiness
- Installation / rollout time: Fabricate ring modules in 4 weeks, ship and bolt together within 72 hours during harbour downtime, solar + IoT commissioning 3 days.
- Staff & SOP notes: 1 harbour ops lead, 2 cold technicians, 6 SHG women for packing + QA; remote NOC monitors temperature & flood alerts.
- IoT / monitoring metrics: Temperature variance, battery reserve hours, insurance trigger logs, daily ONDC order volume.
